Statement Bridge

Convert a bank statement PDF into a CSV file - entirely in your browser. Your statement is never uploaded anywhere.

Early version. This recognizes Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, U.S. Bank, PNC, and Ally statements directly, with a generic date / description / amount parser as a fallback for anything else - it does not yet know every bank's exact format. Bank of America and Ally have each been checked against a real statement from their own bank; Chase, Wells Fargo, U.S. Bank, and PNC have not yet, so double-check those results with extra care. Export to CSV, QBO (QuickBooks), or Xero's CSV format. Always check the results below against your original statement before using them anywhere. This tool does not give tax or accounting advice.
